**Page 1 – Production Overview**

**KPIs**
- Total operators count
- Total batches count
- Total product count
- Total flavor count
- Total production time

**Filter:** by month

**Questions**
1. How much production time did each product take out of the total production time?
2. For each product, which operators took part in making it, and what was their average production time?
3. Which specific products generate the highest excess minutes?
4. How many batches did each operator complete?

**Page 2 – Downtime Analysis**

**KPIs**
- Total production time
- Total downtime

**Filter:** by month

**Questions**
1. What are the top 5 downtime factors by time lost?
2. What are the bottom 5 downtime factors by time lost?
3. How does downtime caused by operator error compare with downtime from other causes, in minutes?
4. For each product, which downtime types occurred, were they caused by the operator or not, and how long did each take?

**Page 3 – Operator Performance**

**KPIs**
- Total production time
- Total downtime
- Total operator error time
- Average batch duration per operator

**Filter:** by month

**Questions**
1. Over time, which operator has the highest efficiency? (Operator performance over time.)
2. How much downtime did each operator cause?
3. What is the relationship between the number of batches an operator handled and their downtime? In other words, does a heavier workload lead to more downtime?
